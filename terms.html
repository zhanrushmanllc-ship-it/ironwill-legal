3. Your commitment and how penalties work

3.1 Agreement in advance. When you create a goal or change its rule, you agree that if you miss your goal, we will charge your payment method according to that rule. New goals and rule changes take effect the next day.

3.2 App-limit goals. When you reach your daily limit, the apps you selected are blocked (a "shield"). You may tap "Continue (accept charge)" to keep using them. Each extra minute is then charged at your price, up to your daily cap. If you tap "Close", the apps stay blocked and you are not charged. We charge for extra minutes only if you tapped "Continue". We keep a record of each such confirmation. When the daily cap is reached, the apps stay blocked until midnight.

3.3 Exercise goals. If your day's total is below your target, you are charged per missing unit, up to your daily cap. Only data Apple Health records from Apple devices counts. Manual entries and data from other apps do not count.

3.4 When charges happen. The app settles each day at about 3:00 a.m. in your goal's time zone. We charge at most once a day, for the previous day's total. Charges are processed by Stripe and appear on your card statement under the name shown on your receipt.

3.5 Failed payments. If a charge fails, we retry it 1, 3 and 5 days later. If it still fails, your account becomes past due: no new charges are made, and you cannot start a new goal until the balance is paid.

3.6 Days that are not charged. A day is not charged if Screen Time or Apple Health access was off, or if the app could not measure it. Such days are "unmonitored". Unmonitored days may break your 21-day challenge.

3.7 Stopping. You can pause or delete your goal at any time; this takes effect the next day. You can turn off the app's Screen Time access in iOS Settings at any time; this takes effect immediately. Penalties already incurred remain payable.
