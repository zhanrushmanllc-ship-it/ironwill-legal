6. Drawing and odds

The drawing for a Period takes place on the first day of the following month, at or after 12:00 p.m. UTC. Winners are selected at random from all eligible entries received for that Period, using a procedure anyone can check:

  1. When the drawing starts, Sponsor's server records a SHA-256 fingerprint of the sorted list of eligible entry IDs. Entry IDs are random and contain no personal information. Mail entries are assigned IDs in the same way.
  2. Only after that fingerprint is recorded, the server generates a random seed. The eligible entries are shuffled with that seed using the algorithm published at https://zhanrushmanllc-ship-it.github.io/ironwill-legal/draw-algorithm.html. The first entries in the shuffled order win, one for each Prize; the entries after them are alternates, in order.
  3. After Sponsor's staff confirm the result, the fingerprint and the seed are shown in the app's Draw tab, and the full list of entry IDs, the fingerprint, the seed and the alternates are emailed to anyone who asks at support@zhanrushmanllc.com, so anyone can recompute the result.

Sponsor's staff check the result before winners are notified. Sponsor's decisions on all matters relating to this sweepstakes are final.

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received for the Period.

A potential winner forfeits the Prize if they do not complete these steps in time, are found ineligible or in breach of these Rules, or if a notification or payment is returned as undeliverable after reasonable attempts. A forfeited Prize goes to the next alternate in the shuffled order, under the same claim requirements, up to three (3) alternates per Prize. Prizes are paid within thirty (30) days after the claim is complete. No publicity release is required.
