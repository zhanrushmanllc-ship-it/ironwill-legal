Lock In, Son — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026
Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Zhan&Rushman LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"), collects through the Lock In, Son app (called 最严厉的爸爸 and other localized names in some App Stores) and related services (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

The Service is for adults only (18 or older, or the age of majority where you live). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

1. The short version

  • We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Screen Time: we never learn which apps you chose or how you use them. Your app selection stays on your iPhone. We receive only each day's result: whether you kept your limit, how many minutes you went over, and the resulting penalty.
  • Apple Health: we only read steps and workouts, and never write to Apple Health. Individual samples stay on your iPhone. We receive only the day's total. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared for those purposes.
  • Payments: your card number goes directly to our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store it.
  • You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time.

  • Individual Apple Health samples, their sources, or your other health data.
  • Your precise or approximate location (the app does not use Location Services).
  • Your photos (the app can only add a progress image to your library when you choose "Save Image").
  • Your contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  1. provide the Service: run your account, goals and challenges, settle each day, and show your progress;
  2. charge the penalties you agreed to in advance, send receipts, retry failed charges, and handle appeals and refunds;
  3. run the monthly sweepstakes: determine eligibility, draw and check winners, pay prizes, and meet tax-reporting duties;
  4. send notifications you have allowed, and texts to a supervisor who has accepted your invitation;
  5. keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems;
  6. comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not use health or fitness information for marketing or data mining.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you use the Service from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (section 3, items 1–4); your consent for health data, notifications and supervisor texts, which you can withdraw at any time; our legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention (item 5); and our legal obligations (item 6).

Your supervisor receives texts that show the name you gave them and: that you missed your goal on a given day and by how much (minutes over your app limit, or steps or workout minutes short); that you chose to keep using your limited apps past the limit, with your per-minute price and that day's cap; and that you completed a 21-day challenge. Winners: if someone asks for a month's winners list as the Official Rules allow, we give the winners' first names and states. The public draw verification list uses random entry IDs, never names or account details.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account data: while your account is active. If you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted 30 days later.
  • Charge and confirmation records: after deletion, kept for 7 years in de-identified form (without your account ID or contact details) for accounting, tax and payment-dispute purposes.
  • Prize and tax records: 7 years after the year the prize was paid.
  • Backups: deleted information may remain in backups for up to 35 days.
  • We may keep information longer where the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings > Account > Your data. We email a download link to your receipt email.
  • Delete: Settings > Account > Delete account. If you have unpaid penalties, the app asks you to pay them first.
  • Correct: you can change your profile, receipt email and goals in the app, or contact us.
  • Health and Screen Time access: you can turn these off at any time in iOS Settings. Days without access are recorded as unmonitored and are not charged, but your challenge progress may be lost.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iOS Settings or in the app.
  • Supervisor texts: your supervisor can reply STOP to stop all texts.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Texas, other U.S. states, the EEA or the UK), you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete or port your information, and to object to or restrict its use. To exercise these rights, use the in-app tools or email support@zhanrushmanllc.com. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may also complain to your state attorney general or local data-protection authority.

8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and stored with Google Cloud. Access is limited to people who need it to run the Service. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details safe.

9. International transfers

We are based in the United States and store data in the United States (Google Cloud region nam5). If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information is transferred there. Where the law requires a safeguard for this transfer, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and their UK and Swiss equivalents, in our agreements with our service providers.

10. Changes

If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
