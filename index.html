Lock In, Son — Support

Lock In, Son helps you keep a daily goal by committing in advance to a penalty if you miss it. Finish a 21-day challenge to enter a free monthly draw.

Need help? Email support@zhanrushmanllc.com. Tell us your iPhone model, the iOS version and what happened.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A charge looks wrong. Open the charge in the app and tap Appeal within 7 days. A person reviews every appeal, and approved appeals are refunded to your card. See the Terms of Service.

How do I stop? Pause or delete your goal in the app; this takes effect the next day. Turning off Screen Time access in iOS Settings stops monitoring right away.

Do I need a card? Only for a goal with penalties. A goal with a $0 rule never charges you and needs no card.

How do I enter the monthly draw? Finish a 21-day challenge, or enter by mail. It's free either way. See the Official Rules.

My supervisor wants to stop the texts. They can reply STOP to any message.

How do I export or delete my data? In the app, open Settings, then Account. See the Privacy Policy.
